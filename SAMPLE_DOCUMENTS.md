## 3. FAQ Document (hotel_faq.txt)

```
LUXURY HOTEL - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What time is check-in and check-out?
A: Check-in is at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available upon request for an additional fee.

Q: Do you offer free WiFi?
A: Yes! High-speed WiFi is complimentary throughout the entire hotel for all guests.

Q: Is breakfast included?
A: Yes, we offer complimentary breakfast daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM in The Grand Restaurant.

Q: Do you have parking?
A: Yes, we offer both valet parking ($35/day) and self-parking ($25/day). Spaces are subject to availability.

Q: Are pets allowed?
A: Yes! We welcome up to 2 pets per room (max 50 lbs each) for a fee of $50 per pet per night.

Q: Is there a fitness center?
A: Yes, our fitness center is available 24/7 and includes cardio equipment, weights, and yoga accessories.

Q: Do you have a pool?
A: Yes, we have a heated outdoor pool open from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily.

Q: Is there a cancellation fee?
A: You can cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before check-in. Cancellations within 48 hours will incur a charge of one night's stay.

Q: Do you offer airport shuttle service?
A: Yes, airport shuttle service is available for $25 per person. Please book at least 24 hours in advance.

Q: What restaurants are on-site?
A: We have The Grand Restaurant for fine dining and the Lobby Lounge for casual dining and drinks. Room service is also available.

Q: Do you have accessible rooms?
A: Yes, we have ADA-compliant rooms with wheelchair accessibility. Please contact us when booking to ensure availability.

Q: Can I request extra towels or pillows?
A: Absolutely! Contact housekeeping or the front desk anytime, and we'll be happy to provide additional amenities.

Q: Is there a business center?
A: Yes, our 24/7 business center offers computers, printing, copying, and other office services.

Q: What's nearby the hotel?
A: We're located in the heart of downtown, within walking distance of shopping, dining, and the Convention Center. The beach is 10 minutes away by car.

Q: Do you have meeting rooms?
A: Yes, we have 5 meeting rooms that can accommodate 10-100 people, with full AV equipment and catering services available.

Q: Is smoking allowed?
A: Our hotel is 100% non-smoking. Smoking is only permitted in designated outdoor areas. A $250 cleaning fee applies for violations.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover.

Q: Do you have room service?
A: Yes, room service is available from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM with a full menu selection.

Q: Is there a security deposit?
A: Yes, we hold a refundable security deposit of $100 during your stay, which is released upon checkout if there are no damages.

Q: Can you help book tours or activities?
A: Yes! Our concierge team is happy to help arrange tours, activities, restaurant reservations, and more.
```

## How to Use These Documents

1. Copy each section into separate .txt files
2. Login to the admin panel at http://localhost:3000/admin/login
3. Navigate to the Documents section
4. Upload each file
5. Wait for processing to complete
6. Test the chatbot by asking questions from the documents
